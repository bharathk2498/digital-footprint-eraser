About Digital Footprint Eraser

Privacy-First Design

Digital Footprint Eraser is built with privacy as the foundational principle. All processing happens locally on your device - no data is ever transmitted to external servers.

Key Features

  • 🍪 Comprehensive cookie and tracker cleanup
  • 🏢 Data broker removal from 500+ sites
  • 📱 Social media privacy optimization
  • 🔍 Real-time digital footprint scanning
  • 🛡️ Advanced fingerprinting protection
  • 🔒 100% local processing
