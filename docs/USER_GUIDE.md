## 📋 Features Overview

### 🍪 Cookie & Tracker Cleanup
**Purpose**: Remove tracking cookies and browsing data that companies use to monitor your online activity.

**What it cleans**:
- First-party and third-party cookies
- Local storage data
- Session storage
- IndexedDB databases
- Browser cache (where possible)

**How to use**:
1. Navigate to the Cookie Cleanup section
2. Select cleanup options (all recommended by default)
3. Click "Clean Cookies Now"
4. Review the cleanup results

### 🏢 Data Broker Removal
**Purpose**: Generate removal requests for 500+ data broker websites that sell your personal information.

**What it includes**:
- People search engines (247 sites)

**How to use**:
1. Fill in your information (stays on your device)

3. Download the generated email templates

**Purpose**: Get step-by-step instructions to optimize privacy settings across major social platforms.

**Supported platforms**:
- Facebook/Meta
- Instagram
- Twitter/X
- LinkedIn
- TikTok
- YouTube

**How to use**:
1. Click "Optimize" next to any platform
2. Follow the detailed instructions in the popup
3. Apply settings on each platform manually

**Purpose**: Analyze your current digital privacy exposure and get a personalized privacy score.

**What it analyzes**:
- Browser privacy settings

**How to use**:
1. Click "Scan Digital Footprint"
2. Wait for the analysis (30-60 seconds)
3. Review your privacy score and exposure level
4. Follow the personalized recommendations

## 🛡️ Best Practices

### Daily Privacy Habits
- **Use Private Browsing**: For sensitive activities
- **Clear Data Weekly**: Regular cookie cleanup
- **Check App Permissions**: Monthly review
- **Update Privacy Settings**: Quarterly check

### Browser Recommendations
**Chrome Users**:
- Enable "Send a Do Not Track request"
- Block third-party cookies
- Disable "Make searches and browsing better"
- Turn off "Help improve Chrome's features"

**Firefox Users** (Recommended):
- Set Enhanced Tracking Protection to "Strict"
- Enable "Delete cookies when Firefox is closed"
- Use "Send websites a Do Not Track signal"
- Disable data collection options

**Safari Users**:
- Enable "Prevent cross-site tracking"
- Block all cookies or third-party cookies
- Enable "Hide IP address from trackers"
- Turn on "Private Relay" if available

**Edge Users**:
- Set Tracking prevention to "Strict"  
- Enable "Send Do Not Track requests"
- Block third-party cookies
- Disable telemetry options

### Browser Extensions (Recommended)
- **uBlock Origin**: Comprehensive ad/tracker blocking
- **Privacy Badger**: EFF's tracker protection
- **DuckDuckGo Privacy Essentials**: Search + privacy
- **ClearURLs**: Remove tracking parameters

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**"Cleanup had limited success"**
- **Cause**: Browser security restrictions
- **Solution**: Follow manual cleanup instructions provided
- **Prevention**: Use less restrictive browser settings

**"Some features not available"**
- **Cause**: Private browsing or strict security settings
- **Solution**: Temporarily use regular browsing mode
- **Alternative**: Use manual procedures in our guides

**"Privacy score seems inaccurate"**
- **Cause**: Browser extensions affecting analysis
- **Solution**: Temporarily disable extensions and re-scan
- **Note**: Some discrepancies are normal

**"Can't download reports"**
- **Cause**: Browser download restrictions
- **Solution**: Check browser download permissions
- **Workaround**: Copy report content manually
