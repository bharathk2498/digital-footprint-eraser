# Privacy Policy

**Digital Footprint Eraser**  
*Effective Date: July 20, 2025*

## Our Commitment to Privacy

Digital Footprint Eraser is built with privacy as the foundational principle. This tool is designed to **enhance your privacy**, not compromise it.

## Data Collection & Processing

### What We DON'T Collect
- ❌ **No personal information is transmitted** to our servers
- ❌ **No browsing data is stored** remotely  
- ❌ **No analytics or tracking** of your usage
- ❌ **No cookies are set** by our application
- ❌ **No user accounts or registration** required

### Local Processing Only
✅ **100% Local Processing**: All data analysis and cleanup happens entirely within your browser  
✅ **Zero Data Transmission**: Your information never leaves your device  
✅ **No External Dependencies**: Core functionality works completely offline  
✅ **Open Source**: Complete transparency - inspect our code anytime

## How the Tool Works

### Cookie & Storage Cleanup
- Scans and removes cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage locally
- No data about your cookies is transmitted anywhere
- Cleanup happens entirely within your browser's security sandbox

### Data Broker Analysis
- Provides pre-compiled list of data broker websites
- Generates removal email templates locally
- Your personal information stays on your device

### Social Media Privacy
- Displays step-by-step instructions for privacy settings
- No integration with social media APIs
- No tracking of which platforms you use

### Digital Footprint Scanner
- Analyzes browser settings and local storage patterns
- All analysis happens client-side using JavaScript
- Results are displayed locally and never transmitted

## Data You Control

### Information You Provide
When using the data broker removal feature, you may enter:
- Name, email, phone number, address

**Important**: This information is:
- Stored only in your browser's local memory
- Used only to generate removal request templates  
- Never transmitted to our servers
- Cleared when you close your browser (unless you save reports locally)

### Generated Reports
- Privacy reports are generated locally in your browser
- Downloaded directly to your device as HTML/text files
- No copies stored on remote servers

## Third-Party Services

### External Links
Our application provides links to:
- Data broker websites (for manual removal)
- Social media privacy settings pages
- Documentation and support resources

**Note**: When you visit external links, you're subject to those sites' privacy policies.

### Font & Icon Libraries
We use:
- Font Awesome icons (loaded from CDN)
- Google Fonts (if enabled in your browser)

These are loaded for UI purposes only and don't impact your privacy analysis.

## Browser Permissions

### Required Permissions
- **Storage Access**: To read and clear your local browser data

### Optional Permissions

All permissions are used exclusively for privacy protection features.
